      About BrowserX
      
          
      
            An ULTRA-optimised Chromium browser, C++20 on CEF, with five performance modes plus
            Auto-Pilot and a tracker shield. This preview page is served by
            preview/serve.py and reads the mode engine directly, so the numbers you
            click through are the numbers the browser will apply.

      Two editions, one core

            src/core through a small JNI bridge, so a rule fixed here is fixed in both.
